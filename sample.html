Sample

CSHE uses a multi-stage household sample covering urban and rural areas. Monthly waves follow a repeating panel with limited refreshment so that both repeated-respondent and repeated-cross-section analyses are possible.

ItemDescription
Target populationHouseholds and adult decision-makers
FrequencyMonthly
Typical wave sizeAbout 3,000 respondents
DesignMulti-stage sampling with panel follow-up
WeightsSampling weights released with each wave

Age, education, urban–rural, and regional breakdowns are summarized in the user guide. Users should apply the provided weights unless a specific unweighted analysis is required.
