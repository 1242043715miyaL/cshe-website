About the Project

The China Survey of Household Expectations (CSHE) collects high-frequency household data on how people form and update expectations. The survey is intended for research on inflation, labor-market outlook, consumption plans, and housing-market beliefs, and for work that combines survey expectations with other micro or macro series.

Each monthly wave covers a core block of expectation questions that is kept as stable as possible, plus rotating modules. Rotating content may include information treatments, vignettes, or one-off items on policy or prices. After fieldwork, responses are checked against the questionnaire dictionary, labeled, and released as a wave file and as an updated panel.

Typical modules include: household and respondent background; income and employment; inflation perceptions and expectations; consumption and saving plans; housing and assets; and interviewer observations. Cross-wave correspondences are documented in the codebook so that users can build consistent time series.

Data are distributed by application. Please read the user guide and the latest release note before analysis, and request access on the Contact Us page.
